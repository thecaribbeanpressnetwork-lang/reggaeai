# ReggaeAI Brand Identity

## Brand position
ReggaeAI is the public Caribbean AI music platform. Shot Call Records remains the operating/release intelligence behind the scenes and must not dominate the public identity.

## Core mark
An original geometric winged lion. It signals authority, motion, guardianship and Caribbean creative power without copying legacy BOSS artwork, sports-team heraldry or militaristic imagery.

## Wordmark
REGGAEAI in uppercase. REGGAE uses warm bone/ivory; AI uses metallic gold. The mark may appear alone as an avatar/favicon, while the full wordmark is preferred in navigation, hero and formal brand uses.

## Palette
- Obsidian: #090806
- Charcoal: #11100D
- Warm Bone: #F4EFE4
- ReggaeAI Gold: #D6B45C
- Deep Gold: #9F7427
- Restrained Caribbean Red: #8F241F
- Restrained Caribbean Green: #1F5B3D
- Restrained Caribbean Sun: #D7A91F

Gold is primary. Red/green/yellow are accents only, never the dominant visual treatment.

## Surface language
Premium dark textured surfaces, weathered warmth, restrained metallic detail, cinematic contrast, and music artwork as the visual hero. Avoid generic glass dashboards, tourist/beach clichés, palms, guns, bullets, crosshairs, gangster imagery, Rastafari cosplay and copied heraldry.

## Typography
UI: clean modern sans serif. Editorial/hero: high-contrast serif. The combination should feel like premium music publishing rather than an AI utility dashboard.

## Brand line
CARIBBEAN SOUND · FUTURE INTELLIGENCE

## Design rule
Every state—home, search, player, upload, import, login, error, empty, loading, account, admin and mobile—must look intentionally branded. Functionality must never degrade the visual system into developer tooling.

## Forbidden carryovers
Do not use BOSS DISTRIBUTION text, legacy company names, copied lion artwork, weapons imagery, or any visual that suggests Shot Call owns third-party creator uploads by default.
